## Recommendation: #1 Supersystems
- Ep1 insights: 97% men, builders, India and the US. A PC build is the build ritual this audience already watches and shares.
- Closest to Nate's house (parts going in = construction) but new, with a payoff frame: the RGB turns on.
- Copyable: every viewer's city has a PC builder selling on a marketplace with no site. That is the "learn it, send it to a friend" angle.
- Risk: AI video can melt small parts (cables, pins). Keep the camera fixed, 3/4 view, big parts only; test one 5 s clip before a full chain.

Runner-up for a non-tech crowd: #3 The Visual Lab (simplest AI shot, cars are well modelled). Literal "learn and share" business: #6 Meso, but its customers skew away from Henry's viewers.

## Before anything goes public
Ask the owner. Use their real photos or label generated visuals as concept. Do not show their phone number or prices without a yes.
