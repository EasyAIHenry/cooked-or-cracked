## Pass 3 — style (before any graphic)
Use the design style from my project "AI Editing Guide — Instagram Reel": torn ruled paper cards, ink #171411, paper #FFFEFA, accent #DF825F, Fraunces 900 for numbers and headlines, Kalam for handwritten labels, pop-in with a small overshoot and 1 to 3 degree tilt, no fades, no pulsing while a card sits. Apply this to every graphic below. Do not use Arial or Helvetica.

Add these motion graphics on top of the existing footage without changing the cut underneath. Each one is transparent, editable, and lands on the spoken word.
