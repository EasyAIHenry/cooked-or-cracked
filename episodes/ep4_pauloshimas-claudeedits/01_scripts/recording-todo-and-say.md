# Ep4 recording brief (his page 04 "Film it right", plus our series rules)

## Pre-flight (5 min)
- [ ] Tripod, phone locked. It never moves, so effects stay locked to you.
- [ ] Horizontal 16:9. Paulo shoots horizontal: it gives Claude room to reframe and it's how he gets the "edited on top, ORIGINAL underneath" look. (Vertical works too if you'd rather keep the series framing.)
- [ ] HDR is fine. Your iPhone HLG 50 fps footage rendered through HyperFrames with matching colours (tested on 6 s of the Ep3 take).
- [ ] Light on your face. Step 1 to 2 m away from the wall: a clean edge gives a clean cutout.
- [ ] Quiet room, mic close. Whisper hears better.
- [ ] Have on hand: nothing to hold. The Claude logo is a file.
- [ ] Test shot: film 5 s, drop it in `~/coc-ep4-test/test.mp4`, run his prompt:
  ```
  Here is a 5-second test clip, test.mp4. Pull a few frames and check the shot before I record the real take: is my face well lit, is the background far enough to cut me out cleanly, and is anything important too close to the edges? Tell me exactly what to fix.
  ```

## Take A: the test (about 60 s of talking)
Say each pointer from `script-ep4.md` Part A. While you talk:
- One request per sentence. Short pause after each.
- On "put the Claude logo right here": open hand, palm up, hold still 1 s.
- On "throw it at the camera": flinch. Look where the effect lands and react.
- On "hide my layer": stand still for 1 s.
- Say the CTA slowly (it's in Take B).
- At the end: stay silent 2 s, step out of frame, 2 s of the empty room. This clean plate is what lets Claude hide you.
Name the file `take.mp4`.

- Record: the first prompt, the beat sheet, the first preview, each notes round, the render finishing.
