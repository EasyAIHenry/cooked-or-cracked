how it works

One skill, six clips, one finished video

A skill is a set of instructions Claude follows. This one researches your topic and writes six narration lines, one image prompt for the look and one video prompt per 10-second scene. Your generator makes the image and the clips. Claude records the narrator and joins everything into one 60-second MP4.

set up once, about 20 minutes
1

Two accounts. Claude Pro, US$20 a month, at claude.com/pricing. A Higgsfield plan with Seedance 2.5, at higgsfield.ai/pricing. The test used Ultimate, US$39 a month billed yearly.

2

Get the skill. Comment PYNK on @mr.pynk's reel. His page DMs a link to a Notion page; a free Notion account unlocks the download. Unzip it. You get a folder called Vox Animations.

Spending. The folder tells Claude never to spend credits on its own. Say you may run the Higgsfield prompts for me and Claude asks before each one. Without the connector you paste each prompt into higgsfield.ai and drag the files back.

1

The ask: say the topic, the arc and the voice

2 minutes

Type what you want as if briefing an editor: the subject, where the story starts and ends, the kind of narrator. Ask for three topics if you have none. In the settings table that follows, reply diorama for the paper look in this guide; the default, mixed, is a bright collage. Length starts at 60 seconds and every 10 seconds is one scene. The skill comes back with a one-line angle and six lines of 18 to 20 words, each built on a checkable fact. Read them out loud once. Free.

the ask "Research the most controversial unsolved mystery cases in the world. Give me three topics. From the beginning to the cliffhanger, and end on the part people might never find out. A narrator that fits the mystery feel." It offered D.B. Cooper, the comet 3I/ATLAS and Amelia Earhart.
receipt Six lines, 116 words, two sources per fact, saved in the project folder for you to check.
2

One image locks the look

3 minutes

The skill writes one image prompt in the paper diorama style and offers three looks. Make one as a 9:16 image with Nano Banana Pro on Higgsfield, 2 credits, about 7 cents. With the connector, say generate the lagoon key. Without it, paste the prompt into Nano Banana Pro at 9:16 on higgsfield.ai, download the image and drag it into the project folder. That picture is attached to every clip prompt, which is why six clips come out as one film. Pick the look that fits the ending; this story ends in the sea, so the blue one won.

4

The narrator

2 minutes

The skill offers three voices: Christopher, Ryan and Jenny. Say samples to hear line one in all three; it saves the files in the project folder. Reply with the name you want and it records all six lines with edge-tts, a free tool that uses Microsoft's online voices. It then checks each take against its 10-second clip and reworks any line that runs short or long. Ryan, a British male voice, fits a mystery. This step costs nothing.

line one, as Ryan reads it "At eight forty-three in the morning, July second, nineteen thirty-seven, Amelia Earhart spoke her last words into a radio."
5

Claude cuts it

3 minutes

Drop the six clips into the clips folder of your project and say done. The skill joins each clip to its line, starts the voice a quarter second in and keeps the clip's own sound low under it. It writes final.mp4 into the project folder at the clips' size, 720 by 1280 at 720p. Then two checks: the loudness fix below, and one watch on your phone with the sound on.

receipt 60 seconds, six scenes, 720 by 1280. On the night: first prompt at 22:50, final.mp4 at 23:30, with the camera rolling between steps.

The six scenes of the finished video. One style image kept them in one look; the orange thread runs through every scene because the brief asked for one object that carries through.

If this happens

presetHiggsfield returns a preset suggestion instead of a clip. Say no and send the same prompt again.
429A rate limit after five clips at once. Send three, wait for them, send three.
shortA narration line shorter than its clip leaves dead air. Ask for two more words in that line.
quietThe first final.mp4 plays low. Ask Claude to normalise the loudness to minus 16 LUFS before you post.
textClips misspell long text. The style stamps one short label a scene, such as 8:43, and those rendered right. Keep sentences in the narration.
1080pWorth the extra US$10 only if the video goes to a TV or YouTube. A phone screen cannot tell.

Who this is for, and who it is not for

use it if

You want an explainer, a history short or a product story: one narrator, one look, a clean cut. You can spend about US$14 a video on a generator you already pay for. Swap in any topic.

skip it if

You need your own face, real footage or a brand's exact logo in the shots. Generated clips drift on text and faces. You want it free. The skill is free; the clips cost about US$14.
