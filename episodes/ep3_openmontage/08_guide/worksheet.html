prompt pack

Seven prompts, in the order you use them

Replace the words in square brackets. Everything else works as written. Type every prompt into the same Claude chat: for three, four, five and six Claude sends the request to Higgsfield through the connector and shows you the result. Prompt seven goes to Gemini with the finished video attached.

1 · research
Give me ten surprising, checkable facts about [the job or topic]. One line each, with the source page for each. Mark the ones most people get wrong. Then tell me which ones would stop a thumb on a phone.
2 · script
Turn these facts into a 60 second script for a vertical video. Nine beats, one idea per line, under twelve words a line. Line one states what people think this job is. The last line pays that off. UK English. No hype words, no questions to the viewer.
3 · style samples
Five images of the same scene, [beat 1 in one sentence], one in each style: flat vector, comedy cartoon, chibi 3D, pulp comic, paper cut-out. Vertical 9:16. Same framing in all five so the styles can be compared.
4 · one still
[the style lock, word for word] Scene: [the still column from the shot plan]. One clear subject, room at the top of the frame for captions, no text in the image. Vertical 9:16.
5 · one motion
Image to video from this still, 5 seconds, steady camera, no new objects. [the motion column from the shot plan]. Everything else stays exactly as drawn.
6 · voice
Read beat one in five different Higgsfield narrator voices so I can pick one. Then, with the voice I pick, one take per beat, calm and warm, natural pace, no music. Name the files by beat number.
7 · the check
[in Gemini, with the video attached] Watch this video and answer: Is every word in the captions on time? Which shots warp or morph, and at what second? Is each fact true, and where does it come from? Does the first five seconds make you want the rest? Rate the voice out of ten. Be specific, no praise.
Order of work: research, script, style and nine stills (a contact sheet is all nine on one page; say yes or regenerate one), animate, voice, then "stitch it". Each step gets a yes from you before the next one spends anything.
