# Art direction — Measured Friction

## Design read

This should feel like a precision workshop discovered inside a dark cinema: tactile enough for makers, exact enough for engineers, and fast enough for a 60–90 second feed. Every movement must explain a force, a fit, or a measurement.

## Palette

- Graphite void: `#0B0D10`
- Warm drawing stock: `#F3EBDD`
- Steel cyan: `#6ED6E7`
- Warning red: `#FF4B3E`
- Machined brass: `#D7A84A`

No gradients except a physically motivated light falloff on material surfaces. No generic purple, neon cyberpunk, or floating glass cards.

## Type

- Headlines: heavy condensed grotesk, uppercase only for impact beats.
- Narration support and labels: restrained neo-grotesk.
- Measurements: monospaced numerals with tabular alignment.

## Motion

Objects move as if they have mass. Measuring jaws snap with a short anticipation, then settle. Exploded parts separate along real assembly axes. Transitions are motivated by a cut, slide, press, or caliper sweep; never decorative wipes.

## Signature device

A thin red **clearance gap** with opposing measurement ticks appears only twice: in the cold open at `0.00`, and at the Act III payoff when the gap opens and the parts finally move. It is not scene scaffolding.

## Caption role

Captions are accessibility subtitles with word-level highlighting. On-screen labels must add information—dimensions, fit names, or short equations—and must not repeat the narrated sentence.

## Scene distinctness rule

Every scene gets a different primary subject: fused pin-and-hole evidence, title card, CAD cross-section, tolerance-zone field, three-fit mechanical triptych, physical test gauge, and episode-two teaser. No hero object persists across adjacent scenes.
