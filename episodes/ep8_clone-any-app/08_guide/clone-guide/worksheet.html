Ten features worth an hour, sized.

Each one is a feature people pay a monthly fee for, cut to a slice that fits one sitting. No brand names needed. "Leave alone" is what the original owns. "Hard part" is where your hour goes.

1

Screen recorder

S

record, stop, get a share link

Rebuild: press record, pick a screen, stop, land on a page with a link you can send.

Leave alone: their CDN and processing at scale, viewer analytics, team libraries.

Hard part: the browser owns the screen picker and needs a real click. Big files in one upload are fine on your machine and wrong on a host that limits request size.

2

Booking link

S

one page where people pick a slot

Rebuild: you set weekly availability, visitors see open slots in their time zone, pick one, both sides get a confirmation.

Leave alone: calendar provider sync at their reliability, SMS reminders, payments.

Hard part: time zones. Store UTC, show the visitor's zone, test a day the clocks change.

3

Link-in-bio page

S

one page of links with click counts

Rebuild: a public page at a short address, an ordered list of links, a click count per link, an edit screen.

Leave alone: their domain's reach, verification badges, referrer breakdowns.

Hard part: counting the click through your own redirect without slowing the visitor. Decide what bots count as.

4

Social post queue

M

write now, post later, one account

Rebuild: a composer, weekly slots, a queue with dates, a status per post, one platform.

Leave alone: their partner access to the platforms, multi-account inboxes, best-time suggestions built on their data.

Hard part: the scheduler, and the platform's developer app approval, which alone can take longer than the hour.

5

Habit tracker

S

tick a box a day, see the streak

Rebuild: a list of habits, a 7 column week grid, tap to tick, a streak count per habit.

Leave alone: wearable integrations, community challenges, cross-device stats.

Hard part: the 7 column grid at phone width, and "today" in the user's time zone.

How to use this list. Pick one. Run the size skill on the real app you pay for and see whether it agrees. If it says L or XL, the slice is too big: cut it until it says S, then run the hour. The skills, the tools and this list live at github.com/EasyAIHenry/one-hour-clone.

Five more, all on your own disk.

These five need no cloud and no platform partner. Each one is a Saturday morning. Start with the one you already pay for.

6

Invoice generator

S

fill a form, get a numbered PDF

Rebuild: client, line items, tax rate, a sequential number, a printable page, PDF through the browser's print.

Leave alone: payment collection, bank reconciliation, tax filing, e-invoicing registrations.

Hard part: the number must never repeat or skip. One counter row inside a transaction.

7

URL shortener with stats

S

short link, clicks per day

Rebuild: paste a long link, get a short one, a page with clicks per day for 30 days.

Leave alone: a trusted short domain, link safety scanning, QR analytics at scale.

Hard part: a redirect that writes a row and still answers in milliseconds. Use a temporary redirect (302) so browsers keep coming back through your counter.

8

Form builder

M

a few field types, a share link, a results table

Rebuild: text, choice and checkbox fields, a public fill page, a results table with CSV export.

Leave alone: template libraries, payment fields, spam protection trained on their traffic.

Hard part: answers for a schema that changes after answers exist. Store each response as JSON with the field list it was filled against.

9

Newsletter signup page

S

an email box, a list, a CSV export

Rebuild: a public page with one input, a subscriber list with dates, an unsubscribe link, CSV export.

Leave alone: sending reputation and deliverability, templates, compliance tooling. Sending is a different feature.

Hard part: double opt-in needs one email. Locally, log the link to the console and click it yourself.

10

Kanban board

S

three columns, drag a card, it stays

Rebuild: one board, three columns, add and edit cards, drag between columns, the order survives a refresh.

Leave alone: real-time multiplayer at their smoothness, automations, the integrations ecosystem.

Hard part: ordering. A sortable position per card, written on every drop. Keyboard move belongs on the must list.

What "S" means here. One flow, one person, your machine, one sitting. Sizes are for the slice as written; the whole app it came from is bigger. Two of the ten (queue, form builder) are M because the editor or the platform approval is bigger than it looks. Sized on 7 October 2026.
