1. **Cut the length in half.** Target 60 to 75 s. The average viewer left at 31 s, and the verdict was at 2:05. Almost nobody saw the verdict.
2. **Speak in the first second.** Ep1 spent 0:00 to 0:06 on the Nate window plus the jingle hold before Henry said a claim. Half the audience left by 0:08. Ep2: Henry's first line starts at 0:00 with the Nate window playing muted beside him. The jingle overlaps the table header pop at 0:03, no silent hold.
3. **Verdict at the midpoint, not the end.** Put the score at 45 to 55% of runtime, then the details. The end is for the CTA and the "screenshot this" frame.
4. **Make the save frame on purpose.** Saves beat likes. Hold a clean summary card (the 5-tool scorecard) for 2 full seconds and say "screenshot this".
5. **Keep the series identity.** 118 follows from 10k views says the format converts. Keep the sting, the pill, the scoreboard, the paper stamps.
6. **Write for India and the US.** Plain English, tool names on screen as text, prices and times as numbers, no local references.
7. **Comment gate stays, keyword changes.** Use a Henry keyword, not Nate's. Ep2: DESIGN. Say it once at 50% and once at the end.
8. **Post at Nate's hour.** He posts 15:04 UTC daily (23:04 Singapore). His audience is awake then, and they are the same people.
