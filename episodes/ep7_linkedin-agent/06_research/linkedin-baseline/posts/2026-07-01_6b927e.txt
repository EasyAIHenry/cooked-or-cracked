I am building a podcast for people who are quietly scared of AI.

It is called Zero Context. The idea is simple. Most people start with no context and no clue where to begin, so the show starts there too. No jargon, no gatekeeping, just two people who use this stuff every day explaining it the way friends would.

I spent the last year rebuilding my own company around AI, fifteen staff down to one with revenue up. The tools were the easy part. What held me back was the fear of looking stupid while learning them.

Zero Context is the show I wish existed when I started.

We are incorporating it properly and recording the first episodes now. Build in public, mistakes and all.

If you have been too embarrassed to ask the basic AI questions, this one is for you. What is the AI question you have been afraid to ask?
