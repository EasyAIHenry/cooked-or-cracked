"I'm a jack of all trades."

Founders say this like it's something to be proud of.

I used to.

But there is a second half to that phrase most people forget.

"Jack of all trades, master of none BRO"

Taking every brief, every format, every client who walks through the door feels like a hustle. 

It feels like maximising opportunity.

What you're actually doing is building a business that stands for nothing.

And a business that stands for nothing is very easy to replace.

At Tristeps, we used to do events, commercials, marketing, social, and anything to keep the doors open.

We said yes to all of it.

And we became expensive generalists.

The moment we got specific, the right clients found us faster.
Pricing got easier.

The work got better.

You can die trying everything and be good at nothing.

Pick the one thing you are genuinely exceptional at.

Build your whole identity around it.

Let the rest go.
