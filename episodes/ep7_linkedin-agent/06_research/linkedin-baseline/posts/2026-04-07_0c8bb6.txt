For three years, I thought the team, company, and I were building this together.

We had meetings. Shared goals. 

Everyone was showing up. Everyone was doing their part.

But somewhere along the way, I became the only one who actually cared what happened next.

Not in a dramatic way.

In a slow, quiet way that you don't notice until you're exhausted.

My partners were doing their jobs.

Executing well within their lanes.

Keeping clients happy, keeping the team fed.

But the vision, the next client, the next capability, the next version of what Tristeps could be?

That was only in my head.

I didn't know I was carrying it alone until the day I stopped pushing.

And nothing moved.

That was the day I realised it wasn't a team problem.

It was a leadership problem.

Mine.
