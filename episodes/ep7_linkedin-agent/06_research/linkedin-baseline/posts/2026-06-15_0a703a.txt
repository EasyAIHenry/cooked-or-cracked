I set my alarm for the early hours of a Saturday (last weekend) to watch football inside the US Embassy. Worth every minute, period.

It was the World Cup watch party, USA against Paraguay, hosted at the Embassy here in Singapore.

The football was the easy part to enjoy. 

The part that caught me was watching a country build its brand in real time. A World Cup is the biggest stage on earth, and a nation that uses it to gather people, tell its story, and earn goodwill is running the same playbook the sharpest brands run. Sport is simply the most powerful medium anyone has found to carry it.

That is the work I think about every day, here on a smaller scale.

I also got to meet some of the people who make these rooms happen. Jessie and Hazizah Osman from the Embassy's Press and Media team on the Public Diplomacy side, and Jorim Kok from FleishmanHillard. Sharp, generous with their time, and genuinely good company, and most importantly, good food.

New faces, a rare room, and a quiet reminder that saying yes early and showing up in person is still one of the most underrated moves in business.

Grateful for the invite. Mornings like this are exactly why I show up.
