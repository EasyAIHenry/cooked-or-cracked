"I turned down tens of thousands of dollars."

That is the first line Gavin Sim said when I asked him what most people get wrong about building a business.

I sat across from him, thinking about the time I said yes to every cheque that came in.

I kept jobs that drained my team. I kept partners who had already checked out. 

I kept an office I could not afford because leaving it felt like failing.

The bill for saying yes to everything was higher than any of those contracts paid.

Gavin drove Uber for 18 months before he built an eight-figure company. 

He knows exactly what it costs to walk away from money you need. 

He did it anyway.

This is Note #01 of Founders Notes. A show for founders who are still in the room, still figuring it out, and tired of pretending they have it all sorted.
