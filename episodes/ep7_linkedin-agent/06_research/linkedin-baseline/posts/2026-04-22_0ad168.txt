The CEO Loneliness regardless what conditions there may be.

At one point, I had equal shares in my own company.

And I still needed permission to move.

Not legally. 

Not structurally.

In my head, I had turned my partners into my bosses.


Every idea I had, I filtered first.

Will they agree? Will they push back? Is it worth the conversation?

I was self-editing before I even started.

That is what thinking like an employee in your own company feels like.

You stop making decisions. 

You start making proposals.

You stop moving. 

You start consulting.

You stop leading. 

You start waiting for consensus (Thoughts of being super trapped at this point)

The moment I caught it, I couldn't unsee it.

I had built a cage out of courtesy.

Getting out of it wasn't about confidence.

It was about reminding myself: this is my company too.

My vision matters. My moves count. My direction is legitimate.

If you're constantly asking for approval in your own business, that's worth sitting with.
