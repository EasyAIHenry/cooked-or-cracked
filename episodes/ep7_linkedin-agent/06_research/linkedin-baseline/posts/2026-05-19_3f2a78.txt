15 people in my rowboat.

Some were rowing hard. Some were rowing in their own direction. A few were quietly tired of the journey.

I spent more energy fighting the boat than steering it. I called it 'managing the team'. The honest name was praying for good weather.

Jun Han Chin sketched it on Note #02 and I felt my last 10 years collapse into one image. Two boats. One full of people rowing out of sync. One driven by a single engine pointed in the right direction.

Relying on the tide is not a business plan. It is a hope dressed as a strategy.

The day I stopped praying for favourable wind is the day I scaled down to one full-time staff and watched revenue double.

If you are still rowing harder while quietly hoping for the right tide, DM me the word ROWBOAT. I will send you the sketch that broke me open.
