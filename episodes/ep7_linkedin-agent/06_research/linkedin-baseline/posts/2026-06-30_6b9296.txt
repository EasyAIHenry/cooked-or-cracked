Meta quietly changed how it counts your views, and most brands have not noticed.

Reach became Viewers. Impressions became Views. A small wording change with a big consequence for how you read your own performance.

Here is what actually matters now. Shares and saves from people who do not follow you are what drive reach. A 17-second average watch time is healthy, not a failure. An engagement rate under three percent is an early warning on account health. And a static intro that just sits there kills your retention in the first second.
