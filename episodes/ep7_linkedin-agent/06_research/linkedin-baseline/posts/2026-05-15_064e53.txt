The people you hire should scare you a little.

Not in a bad way.

In the "I genuinely don't know if I can keep up with this person" way.

When we were growing, my partners hired people they could manage from day one.

People who needed direction. Who needed checking in on? Who needed approval before moving?

They called it building a team.

What they were actually doing was building a support system for themselves.

And I get it. Hiring someone more capable than you is uncomfortable.

It means you can't hide behind knowing more.

It means you have to lead, not just oversee.

But here's the thing about hiring people you can babysit: you'll spend all your time doing exactly that.

The business won't grow. Your time won't free up.

You'll just become a very expensive day carer for adults who were never set up to win.

The hires that changed Tristeps were the ones I wasn't sure I deserved.

People who brought something I couldn't replicate.

People who made me sharpen up just to stay relevant in my own company.

Hire people who make you better.

Everything else is just filling seats.
