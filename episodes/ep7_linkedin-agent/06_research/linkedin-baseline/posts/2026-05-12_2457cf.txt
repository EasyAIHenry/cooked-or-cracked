I joined a workout group six weeks ago.

Not for networking. Not to be seen. Not with any plan attached to it.

I joined because I needed something in my week that had nothing to do with building a business.

The group is a handful of content creators. We meet every Monday at City Hall. We push each other through sessions none of us would finish alone.

Last Monday, with my friend Jimena coaching me through every rep, I did my first muscle up.

I've been running companies since I was 21. I've managed teams of 15. I've sat in boardrooms with Google and Sony. I've made a million and rebuilt from zero.

I had never done a muscle up.

Earning that movement, in a room with no agenda, surrounded by people who wanted nothing from me except to try harder - it broke something open.

I've spent 10 years building relationships with a purpose behind all of them.

Every coffee had a reason. Every connection had a direction.

That room has none of that. Just effort, accountability, and people who show up tired and move anyway.

I don't think I realised how much I needed it until I had it.

Build your professional life with intention.

But leave room for the things that have no ROI.

Those rooms have a way of giving you back something you didn't know you'd lost.
