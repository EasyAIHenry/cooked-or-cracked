I talked a client out of the bigger, easier invoice last month.

They wanted a steady stream of AI-generated social content. I told them AI was the wrong tool for their story. Their brand is human, and it deserved real footage of real people.

I run an AI-heavy studio and I told a client to use less AI. The job is to make the brand win, not to sell the tool I happen to hold.

That instinct is the same one that made me cut my own team from fifteen to one when the model stopped serving the work. You protect the outcome, even when it costs you in the short term.

When everyone is swinging a hammer, the most valuable move is telling a client their problem is not a nail.
