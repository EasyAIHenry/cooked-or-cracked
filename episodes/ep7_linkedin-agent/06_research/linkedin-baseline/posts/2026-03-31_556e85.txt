At 27, I made my first million.

At 32, I was the only one left standing.

Not because the business failed. Because I slowly realised I was the only one actually building it.

The team was there. The revenue was coming in. But somewhere along the way, everyone got comfortable.

Comfortable farming the same clients.

Comfortable staying in their lane. Comfortable letting me carry the vision alone.

And I let it happen. Because I didn't know how to lead it differently.

For years I thought the answer was better people. Better hires. Better partners.

What I didn't see was this: I was the bottleneck. Not them.

The moment I stopped trying to fix the people around me and started fixing how I led, everything changed.

I scaled my full-time team down to 1-2 folks. Revenue doubled.

Not because I worked harder. Because I finally got out of my own way.

I'm Henry. Director and Executive Producer at Tristeps Studios. 10 years in. Google APAC, Sony SEA, McDonald's Singapore, 3M Asia.

On this page, I write for founders and operators who are talented, still stuck in the room, and trying to figure out how to lead without losing what made them good.
