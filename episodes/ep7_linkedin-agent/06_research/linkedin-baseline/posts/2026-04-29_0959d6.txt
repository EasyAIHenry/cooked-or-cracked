A partner once said to me:

"If you want to do this, I'll follow you. But this isn't what I want."

I kept going.

I told myself: That's just how partnerships work. Not everyone has to be equally excited about every direction.

What I didn't hear was the real message.

They were already out.

In their head. In their energy. 

In the room.

That sentence was the ending of a chapter I hadn't finished writing yet.

I should have stopped and had the harder conversation right then.

What do you actually want to build?

Is this still the same company we both signed up for?

Are we still going the same direction?

Instead I kept moving. They stayed put.

And I called that alignment for another eighteen months.

If someone in your business says "I'll follow you but this isn't for me," take it seriously.

That's not support. 

That's an exit with extra steps.
