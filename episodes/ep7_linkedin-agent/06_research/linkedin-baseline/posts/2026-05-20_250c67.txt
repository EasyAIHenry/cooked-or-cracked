I told 30 students at Nanyang JC something last month that I wish someone had said to me at their age.

Money is not your most valuable currency.

Time is.

Money can be made, rebuilt, multiplied. Time cannot.

Every hour spent stuck in a role that isn't growing you. Every year in a partnership that isn't aligned. Every month you delay a decision you already know the answer to.

That's the currency you're actually spending.

I've lost money in business. I got it back.

I've lost time in business. I never got that back.

The bottleneck in most people's careers isn't skill, capital, or opportunity.

It's the moment they realised what they were trading their time for - and decided to keep trading it anyway.

Time is the one thing you cannot buy back for yourself. You can buy other people's time. You can never recover your own.
