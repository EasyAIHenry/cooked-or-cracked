My first client paid me in trade, not cash. I was 20 and had no real idea what I was doing.

I did the work, they gave me something I needed, and that handshake started Tristeps.

I wrote my own non-compete that year because nobody handed me one. I learned pricing by undercharging and feeling the gap in my account. I learned contracts by getting burned on a couple.

None of it looked like a real business from the outside. A poly engineering kid with no polished CV, trading work for favours.

Ten years on, those barter jobs turned into brands you would know by name.

You do not need a clean start. You need to start, then refuse to stay the person who started.
