People ask what a founder's week actually looks like. Here is mine, this week.

Monday I was talking to a second-generation owner about saving her family bak kut teh shop with AI. Tuesday I was on a call with Tesla, planning to premiere a film inside their cars. Wednesday and Thursday I was on a steakhouse floor for two full days, shooting until the grills went cold. In between I taught a psychotherapist to build a second brain, recce'd a commercial in an old shophouse, mapped a teammate's career, and told my co-founders we are dismantling the part of the company that pays the bills.

That is not a highlight reel. That is a normal fortnight now.

Ten years ago all of it would have been one thing, corporate video, over and over. The strange gift of rebuilding lean with AI is range. A one-person studio that can move from a hawker shop to a Tesla showroom to a fine-dining kitchen in the same week, and do each one properly.

Some weeks I do not know what I am. A director, a teacher, a founder, a guy carrying c-stands. Most weeks I am all four before lunch.

I would not trade it. This is the most alive the work has ever felt.

What did your last real week actually look like, under the highlight reel? I read every reply.
