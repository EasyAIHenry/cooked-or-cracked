Everyone who started this company with me has moved on. I have made my peace with that.

One went all in on a different path. One reached a stage where building from scratch was no longer the goal. No blow-ups, just a slow divergence, the quiet kind where good people grow in different directions.

By the time the dust settled I was running Tristeps alone, fifteen staff down to one, and somehow revenue had doubled. At 30 I was the only one left standing. For a long time that felt like loss. Now I read it as the experiment finding its shape.

People are meant to outgrow chapters, including the ones you wrote together. Holding on too tight only slows everyone down.
