Spent this afternoon at SuperAI Singapore in one of the sharpest sessions of the day: “Turning AI Ambition into Business Value at Scale.”

Four operators who actually run things at scale.

Richard Fleming, Senior Partner and Head of APAC AI at Bain & Company, moderating.
, @Charamporn Jotikasthira  Executive Director at Bangkok Bank.

Saj Kumar, who leads Manufacturing for Microsoft Asia.

Chen Peng, CEO of minden.ai, the team behind yuu and its two million members.

Three things I walked out with.

	1.	Execution is the real gap.
Every company in that room has an AI roadmap. Most stay stuck in pilots that never reach the P&L. The ones pulling ahead pick one painful use case, ship it, then earn the right to the next.

	2.	Scale rewards leverage.
A bank serving millions and a factory running agentic AI “co-workers” are chasing the same outcome I once chased at a smaller scale: more output from less, without losing trust or quality.

	3.	The returns hide in the unglamorous work.
The messy data, the customer conversations, the internal docs nobody wants to touch. That is where value sits. Rarely in the demo.

Here is where it got personal.

At 25, I made my first million. At 30, I was the only one left standing.

I took Tristeps from 15 staff to one full-time person and doubled revenue along the way. That period taught me where value actually comes from: leverage and subtraction. More bodies and more noise almost never move the number.

The room spent the afternoon describing, at enterprise scale, the exact lesson that nearly broke me at founder scale.

Scaling with AI is a removal game. Whoever learns what to cut, wins. JUST DO IT GUYS! @SuperAI
