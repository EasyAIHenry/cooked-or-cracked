In 2017, Gavin Sim's marriage almost fell apart.

His dad was diagnosed with cancer the same year.

And somewhere in the middle of all of that, people he trusted in business lied through their teeth to get ahead of him.

Three things. One year.

I've had my own version of this. The year my partners started leaving. The month I nearly couldn't make payroll. The moment I realised I had been carrying the whole thing alone for longer than I admitted.

It doesn't come in a neat sequence. It lands all at once and you just have to stay upright.

What Gavin said next is the part I keep thinking about.

"If I had to lie, cheat, lose my integrity to make more - I feel that I've lost myself."

10 years in business. Every kind of pressure. He never moved that line.

I don't think success is about never breaking. I think it's about knowing exactly what you will not trade, no matter how bad it gets.
