This week I sat down to map out a teammate's career and to hire someone whose whole job is to make me unnecessary.

@Zim Visions has grown from doing tasks to running things, so we built him a real progression track. Not a vague promise, an actual path with more ownership and less of me hovering over it. And separately, I brought on a new hire on a six-month plan whose brief is simple. Take the work that still runs through my hands and own it.

Most founders hire to add capacity. I am hiring to remove myself from the critical path. There is a strange humility in it. You spend years making yourself the person everything depends on, then you spend the next years carefully making that untrue.

AI took the technical load off us first. The cuts, the first drafts, the admin. That freed me to do the thing machines cannot, which is grow people. Sit with them, stretch them, hand them the decisions I used to hoard.

The lean version of Tristeps was about keeping only what matters and building the humans who carry it. It was never about doing everything alone.

If you are the bottleneck in your own company and cannot let go, DM me. Watching Zim outgrow me this year has been the proudest part of the rebuild.
