I played pickleball for the first time last Monday, and I am already planning my comeback.

It was at Pickle and Bones, the rooftop court Adrian Ang built at Somerset. So, of course, the place has character as much as he was a funny lad in his ex meme company. 

It feels less like a gym and more like a hangout that happens to have nets. I asked why the name Pickle and Bones, he casually said: "You know it's like you want to play a game that you won't get bored till you are dead, and it has a nice ring to it".

I went in expecting a gentle warm-up. I left sweaty, slightly addicted, and beaten in good company.

A couple of good friends like rounded out the crew, the kind who make a first try feel easy.

I spend most of my week heads-down building. Mornings like this are a reminder that the network you actually enjoy is the one that lasts.
