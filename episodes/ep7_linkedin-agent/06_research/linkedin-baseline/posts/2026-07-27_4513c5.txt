We have a rule. Seven working days from a client's last post of the month, the report is on their desk.

Not when we get to it. Not when the numbers happen to look good. Seven working days, every month, without being chased.

Last week that meant sitting the team down for the internal run-through first, then walking the client through it properly. What worked, what dropped, what we are changing next month. No spin.

Reporting is the least glamorous part of a retainer and the part that quietly decides whether you keep it. Anyone can make good content. Far fewer agencies can tell you honestly how it performed, on a schedule, in a way a busy client can actually read in five minutes.

When I cut the company to its lean core, this was one of the few processes I refused to loosen. Fewer people meant the systems had to get tighter, not looser.

Clients rarely leave over one bad month. They leave because nobody told them what was happening until it was too late.
