My creative studio has one full-time employee. Me. Five years ago there were fifteen of us.

Revenue is higher now than it was with the full team.

Here is what actually happened. I added up what each role produced against what it cost, and most of the headcount was sitting on work that never reached a client or a dollar. I was paying for motion, not output.

So I rebuilt around a tight core and pushed everything repeatable into AI and a small bench of freelancers I trust. The junior-heavy tasks that used to need five people now run on a system.

Leaner gave me better margins, faster turnarounds, and far less time spent managing people instead of making work.

Before you hire your way out of a bottleneck, map what each seat actually produces. Most bottlenecks are structure, not staffing.
