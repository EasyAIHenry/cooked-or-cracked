At 20 I worked at a place that banned the video team from speaking to the talent.

Everything ran on hierarchy and quiet distrust from the top. I learned the craft there. I also learned exactly what I never wanted to build.

The principle that came out of it has guided everything since, through fifteen staff, through cutting back to one, through doubling revenue alone. Stay only as long as you are still learning. The day the learning stops and the only reason left is the paycheck, you have already overstayed.

I left before it felt safe to. Best call I made that year.

A paycheck is never enough reason to stay somewhere that has stopped growing you.

In a seat that no longer teaches you anything? That is your signal.
