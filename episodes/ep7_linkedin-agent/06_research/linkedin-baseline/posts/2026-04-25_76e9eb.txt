He woke up at 2pm every day for 18 months.

Spent 5 hours building his business. Then drove Grab from 9pm until 5am. Breakfast. Home. Sleep at 7am.

Then it started again.

14 to 15 hours a day. Every single day. For a year and a half.

I think about the version of myself that kept saying I was too tired to start. Too stretched. Too much going on.

Gavin had no income, no plan, and no certainty any of this was going to work. He did it anyway.

The 8-figure company came after the 18 months. Not before.
