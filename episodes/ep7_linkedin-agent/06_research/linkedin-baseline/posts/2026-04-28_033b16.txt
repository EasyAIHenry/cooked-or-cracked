I gave a partner over $150,000 in resources in addition to his salary.

He still left saying he didn't feel valued.

That wasn't a compensation problem. That was someone who had already decided to leave and needed a story to justify it.

I watched it happen slowly. Small things first. Commitments that slipped. Conversations that went sideways. Decisions that didn't add up.

I told myself it was a rough patch. It wasn't.

Gavin Sim said something in our conversation that I wish I had heard five years earlier.

"If you don't show integrity in the small things, you won't show it in the big things. When someone shows me a lack of integrity, I walk away."

The version of me that kept giving chances and adding resources, hoping the situation would turn around - he needed to hear that.

Money doesn't change people. It amplifies them. If the small things were already off, more resources just make the exit more expensive.

I'm not bitter about it. It's the clearest lesson that chapter gave me.
