I capped client revisions at three rounds. Two things happened. The work got sharper, and my team stopped quietly burning out.

We pulled the numbers and over half of client changes were not improving the work. They were breaking the strategy we had agreed at the start. Endless tweaks feel like service. They are usually scope creep wearing a polite face.

Three rounds forces better decisions earlier. The client thinks harder before each note. We protect the idea instead of sanding it down to please everyone.

I learned this the expensive way, running a fifteen-person team into the ground saying yes to everything. Going lean taught me something simple. Boundaries are how you keep the quality you were hired for.
