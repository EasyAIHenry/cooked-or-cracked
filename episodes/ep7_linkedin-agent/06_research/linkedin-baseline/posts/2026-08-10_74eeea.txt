Nine in the morning until the grills went cold. Ten years in, and I am still on a shoot floor for a burger.

Food content is deceptively brutal. A burger has maybe a ninety second window where it looks like the thing you actually crave. After that the bun sags, the sauce dulls, the whole frame dies. So you build the entire day around those windows, which turns a schedule into a countdown.

That is knowledge you cannot brief into someone on the morning of. It lives in a team that has shot this a hundred times.

Everyone assumes AI takes days like this first. Anyone who has stood on a food set knows why it will not. The generated burger can look flawless and still feel like nobody was hungry when they made it.

AI carries our backgrounds, our cutdowns, our first passes. The hot food, the real steam, the timing, the hunger, that stays human.

Lean company. Long shoot days. Still the best part of the job.
