I used to believe that if I worked hard enough and smart enough, the right people would stay.

I was wrong.

Loyalty isn't earned through hard work. It comes from shared alignment. From two people genuinely building toward the same thing.

The partners who left, the teammates who drifted, the relationships that quietly dissolved, none of them left because I didn't work hard enough.

They left because we were never fully going in the same direction.

And honestly, I wasn't fully in their corner either. Not in the way that matters.

When alignment is missing, effort fills the gap temporarily. But it's not sustainable.

Eventually the gap shows up in decisions. In energy. In the direction someone is facing when they think you're not watching.

I stopped trying to hold people through hard work.

I stopped trying to make effort a substitute for shared belief.

Now I build with people who want to go where I'm going. That's the only version of loyalty that holds.
