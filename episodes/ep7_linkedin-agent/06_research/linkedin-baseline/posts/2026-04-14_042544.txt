There was a moment I should have fought for.

A partner told me to stay out of their department.

And I listened.

On paper it made sense. Respect the lanes.

Let people lead their own areas.

But it wasn't about protecting their work.

It was about protecting their comfort.

I respected it. 

Because I didn't want the conflict.

Because I told myself they knew that space better than I did.

Because backing off felt like trust.

It wasn't.

When a partner asks you to stay out, and you have a reason to be in, that is not a healthy boundary. 

That is a warning sign.

The moment you stop asking questions in your own company is the moment someone else starts running it.

I didn't fight for it then.

I paid for that later.
