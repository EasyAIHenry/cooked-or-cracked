45 of my 105 LinkedIn posts this year got flagged as AI.

I ran every one of them through a free AI-detection script, the kind that scores sentence rhythm, stock words and hidden characters. 45 flagged. 40 on review. 20 passed.

The funny part is how many people have told me I must have a ghostwriter.

I don't. It's AI, scheduled through Buffer.

So I looked at what gave the 45 away.

It wasn't em dashes. There were zero across all 105 posts.

It wasn't the usual AI vocabulary either, because that part of the score came back clean on almost every post.

What dragged it down was specificity. Too few numbers. Too few names. Too few real places.

My best-performing posts are the ones with names in them. Gavin and his 18 months of night driving. A lunch at 48 and 50 Niven Road.

So the script and my readers disagree on 45 posts, and this week I'm running a blind poll to find out which of them is right.

How many names and numbers are in your last LinkedIn post?
