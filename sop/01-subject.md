# SOP 01: pick the subject
- Read the newest `tools/lead-scout` report (runs daily 08:13, Apify) or take the reel Henry sends.
- A good subject has a testable claim about a tool: a price, a count, a time, a "does X".
- Skip news, opinion and drama. The series is receipts.
- Create the episode folder from `skills/cooked-or-cracked/references/episode-template/README.md`.
- Name: `DRIVE_Cooked-or-Cracked_Ep<N>_<Creator>-<Topic>`.
