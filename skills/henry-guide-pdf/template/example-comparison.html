how these numbers were gathered

Everything in this table was checked on 25 September 2026 from the public pages linked in each cell. Both companies change prices, credit rates, discounts and plan contents at their own discretion, sometimes weekly, and promotional rates such as launch discounts and cashback end without notice. Treat every figure as a snapshot on that date, not a quote, and confirm on the linked page before you pay. "A month like mine" is an estimate from my own usage at those rates. I am not affiliated with Higgsfield or Kie and I do not earn from these links.
