# Supers library (every MG super the series has used; add new ones after each edit)

Style for all: paper #FFFEFA with ruled lines, ink #171411, accent #DF825F, gold #F2C14E confetti only. Fraunces 900 numbers and words, Kalam 700 labels, Inter 800 captions. Pop-in with overshoot (0.3 to 1.1 to 1 over ~13 frames), 1 to 3 degree tilt, then hold still. Bubble pop on entrance, mouse click on price cards. Reels safe zone y 120 to 1560, clear of the right icon rail. Code for the torn edge and ruled background is in `~/.claude/skills/henry-scrapbook-reel/references/mg-code.md`.
