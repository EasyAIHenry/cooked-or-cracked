# Writing rules for everything in this series (scripts, supers, captions, guides)

The viewer is watching on a phone, on the Reels tab, with the sound on and no context. Every line must be followable on first hearing and readable on first sight.

## Do
- One idea per sentence. Under 12 words spoken, under 6 words on a super.
- Say the number, show the number, at the same moment.
- Name the tool once on screen (text) and once out loud, at the same moment.
- Receipts, not adjectives: "4 cents", "11 minutes", "it found the broken button".
- Plain verbs: works, costs, found, missed, took.
- UK English. First person. Present tense for what the screen shows.
- If Henry's setup caused a failure, say "my setup", not "the tool is broken".
- Dates in the caption and the pinned comment, never in the spoken audio.

## Do not
- No rhetorical reversals: "it is not X, it is Y", "the video says X but the people tell Y". Say the fact.
- No hype words: insane, crazy, game changer, honestly, literally, mind-blowing, shocking.
- No throat-clearing: "so basically", "let me show you", "here is the thing", "quick thing first".
- No questions to the viewer that the video then answers ("Sound familiar?").
- No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no emoji in supers.
- No time-bound words in speech: last week, today, just launched.
- No adjectives before a number. "62 cents", not "a whopping 62 cents".
- No metaphors that need a second read.
- Never the same key phrase on two stamps.

## Self-check before handing anything to Henry
Read every spoken line out loud once. If you needed a breath mid-sentence, split it. If a super needs two lines of Fraunces at 900 weight to fit 900 px wide, shorten it. If a sentence would survive with the adjective removed, remove it.

## Examples (Ep1 lines that worked, from the insights)
- "Higgsfield wants forty-nine dollars a month from me. I spend about eleven." (number, number)
- "Beginner, use Higgsfield. Know what you're doing, use this." (two clauses, one decision)
- "Comment KIE and I'll send the skill." (one action)
